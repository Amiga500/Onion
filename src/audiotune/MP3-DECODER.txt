MP3 decoder (build-time dependency)
===================================
AudioTune uses the single-header minimp3 decoder by lieff and contributors.
Upstream: https://github.com/lieff/minimp3
Licence: CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The build fetches exactly commit:
afb604c06bc8beb145fecd42c0ceb5bda8795144
File: minimp3.h
SHA-256: 5fb296a790734772b65a5514438cc06d14f24e539c13475ff2a7f737681c82c0

The file checksum and upstream revision are published by FreeBSD ports
(audio/minimp3 g20211130). src/fetch-mp3-decoder.sh validates SHA-256
whether the header is already present or freshly downloaded. A mismatch
stops the build. No decoder dependency is needed on the Miyoo device.
