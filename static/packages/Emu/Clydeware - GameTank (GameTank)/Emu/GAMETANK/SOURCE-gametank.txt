  https://github.com/SundownerSport/gametank-libretro
  version 1.0.3, commit b9d0436ba70786912e9ef2dda9b3b3ee8e1c3c13
  (upstream emulator: https://github.com/clydeshaffer/GameTankEmulator)

Built for RedOnion with the Miyoo Mini toolchain (GCC 8.3-2019.03,
arm-linux-gnueabihf):
  CXXFLAGS="-march=armv7-a -mtune=cortex-a7 -mfpu=neon-vfpv4 -mfloat-abi=hard" \
  LDFLAGS="-static-libstdc++ -static-libgcc" \
  make platform=unix CC=arm-linux-gnueabihf-gcc CXX=arm-linux-gnueabihf-g++
then stripped (arm-linux-gnueabihf-strip).
