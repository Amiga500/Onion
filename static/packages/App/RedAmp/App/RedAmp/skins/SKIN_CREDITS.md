# Skin credits

**RedOnion:** CC0 1.0 adaptation of the supplied Breeze Dark artwork, with RedOnion branding
and improved contrast. Generated by `src/skin/gen_redonion.py`.

**Breeze Dark:** the original CC0 `.wsz` supplied for RedAmp, kept byte-for-byte unchanged.

**Graphite:** original CC0 skin distributed with MiniAmp and its source generator.

The formerly bundled `RedAmp-*` themes were Graphite palette variations, not the authentic
similarly named skins by the YTMinamp project. They have been removed instead of being
misrepresented. Frutiger Aero and Slick are intentionally excluded. To fetch original CC0
Ember, VFD, Vaporwave and Material Music skins on an internet-connected machine, run
`src/tools/fetch_cc0_skins.py` from the complete `App/` + `src/` checkout.

YTMinamp project: https://github.com/ergosteur/YTMinamp-Skins

Only its original `bundled-skins/` collection is advertised as CC0; other collections have
restrictions. Do not redistribute other skins without checking their licenses.
